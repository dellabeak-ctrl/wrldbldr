How to Use

Microsettings

Press Simple or Complex in the sidebar to be dealt a microsetting one card at a time. Each card follows your mouse — right-click to rotate it, then click the board to set it down and the next one is dealt. When two are drawn, right-click either to rotate it, then pick one under “Choose One” and the other goes to the tray. Press the button again or Esc to stop early.

Simple Microsetting

Draw one of each card type

Complex Microsetting

  • Draw 1 Region
  • Draw 2 Landmarks (choose 1)
  • Draw 2 Namesakes (choose 1)
  • Draw 1 Origin
  • Draw 2 Attributes (choose 1)
  • Draw 1 Advent

Actions

Moving cards

Click and drag a card to move it along with every card tucked under it that's lower in the hierarchy (Region → Landmark → Namesake → Origin → Attribute → Advent). Right-click and drag to move just that one card.

Rotating & Removing

Hover over a card to reveal a rotate (⟳) and remove (✕) button.

The tray

Drag a card into the empty space in the top bar to set it aside in the tray. Drag tray cards to reorder them, or back onto the map to use them again. A dragged cluster goes in as a stack and comes back out in the same layout; click a stack to fan it out, and click it again to split it into single cards. While dragging any card, drop it on the 🗑 to delete it.

Lock a card

Use the lock button in the top right of each card to lock it in-place on the map

Saving your world

Use the Workspaces panel to save, load, rename, or delete named worlds.

Tips

  • Tuck cards under eachother to show only 1 or 2 cues
  • Create multiple microsettings for a full world map
  • Save your progress frequently
  • Export your workspaces and save them to your desktop to ensure safe keeping!
