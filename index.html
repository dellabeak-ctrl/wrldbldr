How to Use

Microsettings

Simple Microsetting

Draw one of each card type

Complex Microsetting

  • Draw 1 Region
  • Draw 2 Landmarks (choose 1)
  • Draw 2 Namesakes (choose 1)
  • Draw 1 Origin
  • Draw 2 Attributes (choose 1)
  • Draw 1 Advent

Actions

Moving cards

Click and drag a card to move it along with every card tucked under it that's lower in the hierarchy (Region → Landmark → Namesake → Origin → Attribute → Advent). Right-click and drag to move just that one card.

Rotating & Removing

Hover over a card to reveal a rotate (⟳) and remove (✕) button.

Lock a card

Use the lock button in the top right of each card to lock it in-place on the map

Saving your world

Use the Workspaces panel to save, load, rename, or delete named worlds.

Tips

  • Tuck cards under eachother to show only 1 or 2 cues
  • Create multiple microsettings for a full world map
  • Save your progress frequently
  • Export your workspaces and save them to your desktop to ensure safe keeping!
